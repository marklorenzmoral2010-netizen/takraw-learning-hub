Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Sepak Takraw?

Sepak Takraw is a sport played with a ball using the feet, knees, chest, shoulders, and head without using the hands.

2. How many players are on a team?

Each team has three players, also called a Regu.

3. Can beginners play Sepak Takraw?

Yes. Beginners can start by learning the basic skills and practicing regularly.

4. What is the most important basic skill?

The Inside Kick is the foundation of passing and ball control.

5. What equipment is needed?

A Sepak Takraw ball, net, court, sports shoes, and comfortable uniform.

6. Why is teamwork important?

Good communication and teamwork help players defend, pass, and score effectively.

Basic Skills in Sepak Takraw

Mastering the basic skills is the foundation of becoming a great Sepak Takraw player. Practice each skill regularly to improve your control, accuracy, and confidence.



Inside Kick

The inside kick is the most common skill used to receive and pass the ball. Use the inside part of your foot to control the ball accurately.

Outside Kick

The outside kick uses the outer part of the foot. It is useful when the ball comes from difficult angles.

Knee Kick

Lift your knee to keep the ball in the air. This technique helps when the ball is too high for a foot pass.


Heading

Use your forehead to pass or defend the ball. Always keep your eyes on the ball while heading.

Service

The server begins each rally by kicking the ball over the net. A strong and accurate serve gives your team an advantage.

Spike

The spike is an attacking kick used to score points. Players jump and kick the ball powerfully over the net.


Blocking

A block is used to stop or return an opponent's spike. Good timing and jumping ability are important.

Teamwork

Good communication and teamwork help players defend, pass, and attack more effectively.



Practice Tips

Skill Practice Tip
Inside Kick Practice passing with a partner every day.
Outside Kick Improve foot control using both feet.
Knee Kick Keep your balance while lifting your knee.
Heading Watch the ball carefully before making contact.
Service Aim for accuracy before power.
Spike Practice jumping and timing.
Block Work together with your teammates.

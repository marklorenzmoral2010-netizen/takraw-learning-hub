Rules and Regulations

Sepak Takraw follows official rules to ensure fair play and sportsmanship. Players must use skill, teamwork, and discipline throughout the game.


Basic Rules

👥 Team

Each team has three (3) players called a Regu.

⚽ Ball Contact

Players may use only the feet, knees, chest, shoulders, and head. Hands and arms are not allowed.

🏐 Three Touches

Each team is allowed a maximum of three touches before sending the ball over the net.



Official Game Rules

Rule Description
Number of Players 3 players per team
Maximum Touches 3 touches before returning the ball
Scoring 21 points per set
Winning the Match Best of 3 sets
Allowed Body Parts Feet, knees, chest, shoulders, and head only
Not Allowed Using hands or arms during play


Player Responsibilities

🤝 Respect

Show respect to teammates, opponents, coaches, and officials.

💪 Discipline

Follow the rules and always display good sportsmanship.

😊 Fair Play

Play honestly and accept the referee's decisions.

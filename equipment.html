Equipment Used in Sepak Takraw

Every Sepak Takraw player uses standard equipment to ensure a safe and fair game. Below are the essential items used in every match.



🏐 Takraw Ball

The official ball is made of synthetic fiber or woven material. It is lightweight and designed for kicking.

🥅 Net

The net separates the two teams. Players must send the ball over the net without using their hands.

📏 Court

Sepak Takraw is played on a court similar in size to a badminton court.



👕 Uniform

Players wear jerseys, shorts, and comfortable sports clothing during matches.

👟 Shoes

Non-slip indoor or court shoes help players move safely and perform better.



Equipment Summary

Equipment Purpose
Takraw Ball Used for passing, serving, and scoring.
Net Separates both teams during the match.
Court Official playing area.
Uniform Provides comfort and identifies the team.
Sports Shoes Improves grip, movement, and player safety.

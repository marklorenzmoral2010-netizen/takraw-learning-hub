Step-by-Step Sepak Takraw Tutorials

Follow these beginner-friendly tutorials to improve your skills in Sepak Takraw. Practice each lesson regularly until you become confident.



Lesson 1: Warm-Up Exercises

Always start with stretching exercises before playing. Warm-ups help prevent injuries and prepare your muscles for movement.

Lesson 2: Inside Kick

Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Use the inside of your foot to gently pass the ball to your partner. Focus on balance and accuracy.

Lesson 3: Serving

Practice your serve by aiming the ball over the net. Keep your eyes on the ball and swing your kicking leg smoothly.


Lesson 4: Spiking

Learn proper jumping and timing. A strong spike can help your team score points against the opponent.

Lesson 5: Team Practice

Work with your teammates to improve communication, passing, blocking, and teamwork during a match.

About Sepak Takraw

Sepak Takraw is a sport that originated in Southeast Asia and is played by two teams separated by a net. Players use only their feet, knees, chest, shoulders, and head to control and pass the ball. Hands and arms are not allowed during play.


This sport combines football-style kicking skills with volleyball rules, making it one of the most exciting and challenging games. It requires speed, flexibility, teamwork, balance, and quick decision-making.


Purpose of This Website

Takraw Learning Hub was created to help beginners, students, and sports enthusiasts learn the basic rules, skills, and techniques of Sepak Takraw. The website provides easy-to-understand lessons, tutorials, videos, and practice guides for anyone interested in the sport.


Learning Objectives

📘 Learn

Understand the history, rules, and equipment used in Sepak Takraw.

⚽ Practice

Develop fundamental skills such as the inside kick, service, and spike.

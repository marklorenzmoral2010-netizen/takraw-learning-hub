History of Sepak Takraw

Sepak Takraw is one of the oldest traditional sports in Southeast Asia. It was first played hundreds of years ago in countries such as Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and the Philippines. In the past, players stood in a circle and tried to keep the ball in the air without letting it touch the ground.


As the sport became more popular, rules were developed and a net was introduced. This transformed Sepak Takraw into the fast-paced team sport that is played in schools, national competitions, and international tournaments today.


Timeline

1400s

Early versions of Sepak Takraw were played in Southeast Asia using a woven rattan ball.

1940s

Modern rules were introduced, including the use of a net and team competition.

Present

Sepak Takraw is now played around the world and is included in major sporting events such as the Southeast Asian Games.
